Most operational problems aren't dramatic. They're the daily workarounds: the spreadsheet someone has to update twice, the approval that sits in a chat, the stock count that doesn't match, or the customer detail that lives in three places.

ToolsRhino exists to sort out that kind of friction. We map the process first, then build the right combination of software, automation and integrations to make the work easier to see and easier to manage.

We are the specialist systems and automation company within Connectlyx. Connectlyx is the wider business; ToolsRhino is where we concentrate on operational software, AI-enabled workflows and digital products.

A few useful answers

Before we get started.

A project can begin with a clear brief—or simply a problem you want to stop dealing with.

Do you build software around our existing processes?

Yes. We start by understanding how the business works and where the friction is. Where a process needs improvement, we'll discuss that too; the goal is a system people can use, not a digital copy of every workaround.

Can you automate work across the tools we already use?

Often, yes. The options depend on the systems involved, the data they expose and the level of control needed. We'll identify what can be connected reliably before recommending an approach.

Is Gym Command Center available now?

Gym Command Center is in final preparation. Contact us to discuss its progress and early access. We'll share the portal link when it's ready for external users.

How much does a custom management system cost?

It depends on the workflows, user roles, integrations, reporting and delivery stages. We prefer to understand the job first and propose a realistic scope rather than quote a number that may not fit your needs.

How do we start a project?

Send us a short note about your business, the process that needs attention and what you want to improve. We'll use that as the starting point for a focused conversation.
