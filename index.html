Who we are

Technology should fit your business.

ToolsRhino is the specialist technology arm of Connectlyx, focused on making day-to-day business operations simpler, smarter and more measurable. We bring together operational thinking, thoughtful software design and modern AI capabilities to solve the problems that generic tools often leave behind.

A few answers

Good questions lead to better systems.

Not sure where to begin? Here are some of the questions we hear most often. If yours is not here, just ask.

Ask us directly ↗
What does ToolsRhino build?

We focus on custom business management systems, AI-enabled workflow automation, SaaS product development, process digitisation, integrations and operational dashboards. The right combination depends on your goals and existing setup.

Can you build a system for our specific industry?

Yes. We start by learning how your operation works, including its terminology, roles, approvals and reporting needs. We can then shape a solution around the workflows that matter to your team, whether you work in manufacturing, agriculture, healthcare, hospitality, distribution or another sector.

Do we need to know exactly what we want before contacting you?

No. You can start with a business problem: duplicated data entry, delayed reporting, missed follow-ups, poor inventory visibility or too many disconnected tools. We will help clarify the opportunity and define a sensible first step.

Can AI automation work with our existing software?

Often, yes. The options depend on the tools involved and the access they provide. We assess available integrations, APIs and workflow constraints before recommending a practical approach. We also consider permissions, review steps and failure handling.

How long does a project take?

Timelines depend on scope, complexity, integrations and how quickly requirements can be validated. A focused automation or minimum viable product may be delivered in stages, while a broader management platform usually requires a more involved discovery and implementation plan.

Is ToolsRhino part of Connectlyx?

Yes. ToolsRhino is a specialist sister company within the Connectlyx family, focused on AI automation, custom SaaS products and management systems for businesses and industries. You can learn more about our parent company at connectlyx.com.

What happens after launch?

We can help your team onboard, gather feedback and plan improvements. Support and ongoing development arrangements are discussed according to the project and the level of assistance your organisation needs.
