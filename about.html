We build the systems behind businesses that mean business.

ToolsRhino is a specialist sister company of Connectlyx, created to give AI automation, SaaS and management systems the dedicated focus they deserve.

Our story

Born from a pattern we kept seeing.

Working with business owners through Connectlyx, one pattern kept repeating. Capable, hard-working people were running mills, clinics, shops and farms on a tangle of notebooks, spreadsheets and chat threads. Good businesses, held back by tools that never quite fit.

Off-the-shelf software was either too generic or too heavy. Custom software felt out of reach. So we created ToolsRhino: a team devoted to building practical, affordable, intelligent systems that match the way real businesses operate.

The name is deliberate. A rhino is tough, focused and difficult to stop once it is moving. That is the standard we hold our software to.

How we think

Four principles behind every build.

Start with the workflow

We study how your people work before we propose anything. The software follows the business, never the reverse.

Make AI useful first

We would rather automate one boring task perfectly than showcase ten features nobody asked for.

Simple enough for the shop floor

If a new team member cannot use it after a short walkthrough, we go back and simplify it.

Stay after launch

Go-live is the beginning of the relationship. We keep supporting, refining and extending.
