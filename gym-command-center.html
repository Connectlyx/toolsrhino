What is inside

Twelve capabilities, one dashboard.

Everything a gym owner and the front-desk team need to run the business day to day.

Command Center dashboard

Active members, monthly revenue, outstanding balances, attendance, new joiners and expiring memberships at a glance.

Members and memberships

Member records, plans, renewals, replacements and cancellations managed in one place.

Invoices and PDFs

Create invoices, follow their status and generate PDF copies for members.

Payment tracking

Record payments against invoices and see balances update immediately.

Payment reminders

Two configurable reminder dates per invoice, skipped automatically once an invoice is no longer eligible.

Attendance

Record check-ins and review attendance history by member and by day.

Lead management

A Kanban-style pipeline that keeps every enquiry moving until they join or decline.

Trainer records

Keep trainer details and related information alongside the rest of your operation.

Reports and CSV export

Date-range reports for revenue, attendance, overdue payments and leads.

Notifications

In-app alerts for payment events and items that need attention.

Staff roles and access

Owner, manager, receptionist, trainer and accountant roles, designed for the right people to see the right information.

Currency and timezone

Configure each gym’s currency and timezone for the market it operates in.

Attention required

Know what needs doing before you go looking for it.

Expiring memberships, overdue invoices, members who have stopped visiting and leads waiting for a reply usually live in four different places. Gym Command Center brings them into a single view, so the first five minutes of the day are spent acting, not searching.

The goal is simple: fewer forgotten renewals, fewer unpaid balances and fewer enquiries that quietly go cold.

  • Memberships expiring in the next seven days
  • Overdue invoices and the amount outstanding
  • Inactive members who have stopped visiting
  • Leads that need a follow-up
  • Payment events and in-app notifications

FAQ

Gym Command Center questions

Is Gym Command Center available now?

Not yet. It is in its final development and production-readiness phase. We plan to open early access once onboarding, security and the core workflows have been verified. Join the early-access list and we will contact you directly.

Who is it designed for?

Independent gyms and small fitness businesses with one location, recurring memberships and regular payment collection. Fitness studios are a natural fit too. Multi-branch support is on our roadmap.

Can it send WhatsApp reminders?

WhatsApp messaging is planned, but it is not part of the first release. At launch, payment reminders are designed to go out by email, with in-app alerts and the Attention Required view keeping staff informed.

Does it take online payments?

Staff record payments against invoices inside the system. Online payment links, QR payments and gateway processing are on the roadmap and will be added based on customer demand and the markets we serve.

Is each gym’s data kept separate?

Yes, that is how the product is designed. It uses a multi-tenant architecture in which each gym has its own organisation, staff access and settings. Ahead of launch we are verifying data isolation and permissions with dedicated security testing.

Which currencies and countries does it support?

Each gym is configured with its own currency and timezone, so the product can be set up for markets such as Pakistan and the United States. Each client deployment uses a single configured currency.

Can it connect to biometric devices or door access?

Not at the moment. If you rely on a specific device, tell us the model and we will consider it when planning integrations.

What will it cost?

Pricing will be announced at launch. Early-access gyms will hear it from us first.
