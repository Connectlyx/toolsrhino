Rice mill management software built for the way mills really run.

From paddy intake to dispatch, our rice mill management system keeps orders, stock, staff, payments and deliveries in one secure portal, with a bilingual AI assistant that answers questions from your own data.

Book a Demo Talk to our team
The challenge

Where the day-to-day usually leaks time and money.

A rice mill is a chain of handoffs. Paddy arrives, gets weighed, dried, milled, graded, bagged and dispatched, and money moves in both directions at every step. When that chain lives in notebooks, spreadsheets and phone calls, small gaps turn into disputed weights, missing bags and late payments.

Our rice mill software is designed to replace those gaps with a single live record. Every order, stock movement and payment is entered once, visible to the right people, and reflected immediately in your reports. Owners get clarity. Supervisors get less paperwork.

In development

Sound familiar?

  • Weights and bag counts that do not match between the gate, the godown and the invoice
  • Client balances kept in ledgers that only one person fully understands
  • Attendance and daily-wage payroll calculated by hand at month-end
  • No quick answer to “how much stock of each grade do we have today?”
  • Deliveries that depend on phone calls, memory and goodwill
What it covers

Everything a rice mill management operation needs.

Each module is configured around your own workflow, so the portal feels familiar from day one.

Orders and sales

Quotations, orders and invoices linked to the client and the stock they draw from.

Stock by grade and location

Paddy, each rice grade, broken rice, bran and husk, tracked per godown with full movement history.

Client and broker ledgers

Running balances, opening balances and a clear statement for every account.

Staff, attendance and payroll

Daily attendance, overtime and salary runs that calculate themselves.

Deliveries and dispatch

Assign vehicles, record what left the gate and confirm what arrived.

Payment reminders

Overdue balances trigger polite WhatsApp reminders, so nobody has to chase by hand.

Live reports

Stock, sales, receivables and profit by date, client or product, always current.

Bilingual AI assistant

Ask “how much Super Kernel is in stock?” in English or Urdu and get an answer from your data.

FAQ

Rice Mill Management software questions

Is your rice mill management system available now?

It is currently in development. If you would like early access or a tailored version for your mill, contact us and we will talk through your requirements and agree a timeline before any work begins.

Can it track different rice grades and by-products?

Yes. Stock is tracked per item and per location, so paddy, every rice grade, broken rice, bran and husk each keep their own history.

Does it work in Urdu as well as English?

Yes. The portal and the AI assistant support both languages, so owners, accountants and floor supervisors can each use the one they prefer.

Can we bring in our existing records?

Yes. We import client lists, opening balances and stock from your spreadsheets so you begin with accurate numbers rather than a blank slate.
