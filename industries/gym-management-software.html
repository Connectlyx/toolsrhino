Gym management software that shows you what needs attention first.

The challenge

Where the day-to-day usually leaks time and money.

Most gyms do not lose members dramatically. They lose them quietly, through a renewal nobody chased, an invoice that was never followed up or an enquiry that went cold. The information needed to prevent that usually exists, but it is scattered across registers, spreadsheets and chat threads.

Gym Command Center is our gym management software, designed to bring that information together. It connects the whole journey from first enquiry to monthly reporting, and highlights what needs action today.

FAQ

Gym Management software questions

Is Gym Command Center available yet?

Not yet. It is in its final development and production-readiness phase, and we plan to open early access once onboarding, security and core workflows are verified. Join the early-access list to hear first.

Can it remind members about payments?

Yes. Gym Command Center is designed with two configurable payment-reminder dates per invoice. At launch these go out by email, and WhatsApp messaging is on the roadmap.

Does it work for several branches?

Branch management is planned. The first release focuses on independent, single-location gyms, and we will extend to multi-branch businesses once those workflows are fully tested.

Can members pay online through the system?

Staff record payments against invoices today. Payment links, QR payments and gateway processing are planned for later releases.
